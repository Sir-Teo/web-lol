# Lost Chapter and Enlighten

Lost Chapter (`3802`) now grants 40 AP, 300 mana and 10 ability haste. It costs 1200 total gold and sells for 840. Its recipe consumes Amplifying Tome, Sapphire Crystal and Glowing Mote plus 250 gold. Full Data Dragon 16.18.1 and raw CommunityDragon 16.18 records are pinned in [reference/mage-items.json](reference/mage-items.json); both original response hashes were checked. `tools/assets/export-mage-items.py --check` verifies the generated catalog, recipe, stats, tooltip and named passive values. Its original icon is local with source URL, hash and byte size in the asset manifest.

The raw item's `ManaRestorePercent` is 0.2 and `RestorationDuration` is 3, corroborated by the current tooltip. Its retained `MythicItems` ownership group permits one owned item. Only Lost Chapter currently uses that group in this catalog; purchases and snapshots reject duplicate Chapter ownership. Future items in the same source group will need cross-item exclusion, with attention to whether source group names are legacy labels.

## Resource and lifecycle policy

Completing the recipe applies net stats: consumed Sapphire mana remains in the completed item, so the upgrade adds no extra mana or refill; Tome's 20 AP becomes 40 and Mote's 5 haste becomes 10. Other inventory copies remain untouched. Full inventories can complete the recipe when component slots are freed. Invalid purchases are atomic. Existing direct/partial recipe planning also applies.

On each living owner's level increase while Chapter is owned, Enlighten captures 20% of maximum mana after that level's growth. It restores equal portions over the following 180 ticks, alongside ordinary regeneration and clamped to maximum mana. There is no immediate passive refill at the level event. A later level refreshes the single effect using the new maximum and a fresh duration. For multiple levels in one XP award, the final level's refresh replaces earlier ones. Death cancels it, and leveling while dead does not start it. Selling Chapter removes its stats but preserves an already-started restoration and its captured amount.

**Native calibration remains required** for the first restoration callback, per-tick cadence, capture versus dynamic maximum mana, refresh on closely spaced levels, sale persistence and death behavior. The source values prove total fraction and duration, not these lifecycle details. The current policies are explicit and tested, rather than presented as recovered server scripts. Dedicated native particle/audio feedback and a buff-status display are not implemented; the existing mana HUD shows restored resources.

Snapshot 45 stores nullable per-champion `{startTick, endTick, totalMana}`. It rejects future starts, expired or incorrect three-second deadlines, nonpositive amounts and restoration on dead champions. Ownership is not required during restoration because sale persistence is allowed. Mid-effect save restoration exactly matches uninterrupted continuation. Earlier formats are incompatible under the existing ruleset policy.

## Lux purchases and verification

Lux starts Tome and boots, then buys Sapphire and Mote toward Chapter, or completes Chapter directly when its remaining cost is affordable. Completed Chapter suppresses rebuying the consumed Tome. It then progresses through Rods, Deathcap and Wand. This remains a local policy for the available catalog, not a native optimal build or full six-item plan. The existing full-inventory Deathcap regression now starts after Chapter completion and verifies that later recipe independently.

All 595 source/simulation checks, three focused Chrome scenarios, source regeneration and nested-path production smoke pass. New checks cover source fields, full-inventory completion without duplicate mana, unique ownership, exact 180-tick restoration, midway saves, refresh, clamping, death, sale, invalid saves and bot component progression. The browser case purchases through the shop and confirms level-up restoration reaches the mana HUD; existing haste-tooltip and Lux combat browser cases also pass. `artifacts/lost-chapter.png` was inspected.

A twenty-minute headless source-Rift run with eight Ashe and two Lux had no finite/resource violations and exact checkpoint restoration. Both Lux bots owned Chapter. Blue reached level 11 with Chapter, boots, Deathcap and Wand; red reached level 9 with Chapter, boots and two Rods, holding 822.4 gold toward the 1100 completion. No winner was reached. Lux cast E 63, Q 66, W 34, R 10 and Heal 8 times. Source/report hashes and final records are in [reference/lost-chapter-verification.json](reference/lost-chapter-verification.json). This is a deterministic diagnostic, not a balance comparison, full-match completion or rendered-performance measurement. Complete mage upgrades, role AI and production fidelity gates remain open.
