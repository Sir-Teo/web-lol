# Saved Double Strike trails

The private Yi fixture renders both complete authored Passive trail systems.
Each accepted Double Strike child owns one presentation record with two separate
marker decisions. The events do not establish two spell-audio callbacks, and no
new audio is added.

The literal source frames 6 and 9 index identical retained default/event rendered
sample clocks: 0.20000001788139343 and 0.30000001192092896 seconds. Both clips have
56 samples and a rendered duration of 1.8333333730697632 seconds. The current
child cycle scales that clock; each event uses its first exact fixed-step
crossing and the existing next-tick particle birth. Native callback timing and
omitted `mTickDuration` remain uncalibrated.

Marker decisions resolve after the actor's current body animation clock. A marker
admits only while the accepted child owns the current Passive pose. Movement
can suppress the first marker while a resumed stationary pose admits the second;
the skipped first event is not replayed. Q/W cut the child pose while its accepted
gameplay contact can survive. A mid-update death pass records roots without
resolving other actors against stale body poses. Later cuts cap new markers while
already admitted finite particles retain their source lives.

Three verified transports retain all 25 authored mip levels. One PNG is new;
the common ramp and second trail texture reuse primary assets. Both events share
the actual page-owned 1,500-corner primary trail geometry, and both renderers use
one finite-particle sampler. Explicit retries retain verified successes; ordinary
Ashe/Lux startup loads no private passive trail assets or renderer.

Both emitters explicitly author blend 4, full bind and no culling. Their local
and particle-local frame fields remain omitted in the raw reading. The renderer
uses the current full named center-joint position/quaternion with no root fallback.
Source-X reflection, 0.01 VFX units, omitted scale/lookup adapters and local frame
flags remain explicit reconstruction choices. Native placement, inherited rig
scale, shader inputs and frame parity remain uncalibrated.

Schema 161 saves the accepted child clocks/target, separate marker decisions,
pose-cut clock and rolling lossless 16-tick root/visibility window. Histories
survive the live child's removal long enough to finish finite particles. Decoding
checks ordered identities, active-child linkage, decision clocks, exact canonical
root coverage and endpoints, audience and expiry. A complete historical combat
or pose ledger is not saved, so validation cannot independently prove every past
admission or marker decision.

Hidden births stay hidden after reveal. Q hides current bound surfaces. Finite
particles follow known corpse joint poses, and victory drains admitted tails
without advancing the world or deciding future events. Save/Load preserves both
particle groups and exact future world state. Cosmetic sampling consumes no
gameplay random values or entity IDs.
