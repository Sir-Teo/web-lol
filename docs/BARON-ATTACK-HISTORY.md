# Saved Baron attack histories

Save schema 164 retains each released Baron attack using its existing projectile
ID, without allocating gameplay IDs or consuming random numbers. Ruleset:
`9c4d0ece38e7bfd3000ecc5617ccbef954831b064f7c1259bd6d920c89951d4c`.

Flights retain a bounded 90-frame path with absolute distance and historical
visibility. Stopped roots retain their endpoint and subsequent visibility.
Contacts capture the accepted target radius, rig and finalized pose each tick;
death/removal detaches and freezes the pose. These are explicit local playback
policies, not a certification of native ownership after death.

The source-derived conservative bounds are generated by
`tools/assets/export-baron-attack-history-bounds.py`: 87 ticks after flight stop
and 125 ticks after contact. Particle linger remains serialized in the private
program; it is not interpreted as extending natural visible life here.
Strict snapshot validation rejects inconsistent clocks, geometry, identities,
ownership, distances, detached poses and retained lifetimes.

## Verification

- 35 history cases pass, including exact save futures, Flash, source/target
  death, long paths and edited-history rejection.
- Four paired scenarios preserve the old gameplay trajectory for 1,680 ticks,
  ignoring only version, ruleset and the added history field:
  `artifacts/baron-history-gameplay-parity.json`.
- Five browser cases pass, including real UI saves during flight and contact:
  `artifacts/baron-history-browser.log`. TypeScript and production build pass.
- Full unit run: 3,886 pass, three fail. Two are the existing missing Leona
  archive and Lux historical digest gaps. The wave-clock case exceeded its
  unchanged five-second deadline under competing workers; its isolated rerun is
  passed unchanged in isolation (all nine lane cases pass), recorded in
  `artifacts/baron-history-quiet-lane.log`. The cohort resumed without errors.

Native particles are not yet rendered. Launch-mouth pose capture, attachment
transforms, materials and native visual comparison remain work. The current
marker remains provisional. The running 100-seed cohort is frozen to the prior
e20 ruleset; it does not certify schema 164.
