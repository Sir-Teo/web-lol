# Critical attacks and Cloak of Agility

Champions can now buy Cloak of Agility for 600 gold, gaining 15% critical strike chance per copy, and sell it for 420 gold. Chance is derived from each champion's inventory, capped at 100%, and does not change attack damage or other flat stats. The item uses the pinned 16.18.1 icon and a distinct internal ID (`agility`) so it cannot collide with Negatron Cloak (`cloak`).

An eligible champion attack rolls once at windup. Successful rolls are retained in the attack cycle and every resulting projectile, including delayed Q flurry shots. Zero-chance attacks and attacks against structures do not consume a draw. Attack damage is captured at release; selling an item or changing the active animation during travel does not rewrite the payload. The implementation uses the match's saved deterministic PRNG. Independent rolls and their windup placement are reconstruction policies, not a reproduction of native bad-luck protection or its exact random sequence.

Lux's critical attacks deal twice the physical attack damage before armor. Illumination remains separate magic damage and is not multiplied. Critical cycles use the source `crit` clip; ordinary cycles retain the two weighted variants. The pinned critical spell selects `Crit`, the same `Lux_BA_mis` flight effect and a distinct `Lux_BA_tar_crit` hit effect. That native critical hit effect is not yet reconstructed; flight attachment and native release timing also remain unfinished.

Ashe's pinned passive calculates ordinary attack damage from crit chance instead of granting extra damage on individual successful crits. With the implemented item set, its multiplier is `1 + critical chance`. This applies to each Q damage payload as well. A successful critical roll instead triggers the existing empowered slow and decay, once on the first flurry impact. Her ordinary crit animation uses the source `crit` clip, while empowered Q keeps its source Q animation. Farming estimates include her guaranteed crit-chance damage but do not assume a random Lux crit. Infinity Edge now supplies an additive critical-damage modifier; see the implementation below. Other critical item interactions remain unfinished.

Schema 53 stores optional successful-roll flags on attack cycles and projectiles. It rejects invalid flag values, non-champion critical cycles, critical spell projectiles and invalid critical projectile sources. The existing strict simulation fingerprint and schema rules reject saves from previous builds. Ordinary attacks without crit items retain their old data shape and do not advance the PRNG.

Source records, URLs and hashes are in `reference/critical-attacks.json`. The Lux character pins establish the ordinary multiplier of 2; the existing Ashe passive pin establishes its damage formula and empowered slow. The current passive no longer requires a previously slowed target, consistent with Riot's [25.S1.1 change](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/news/game-updates/patch-25-s1-1-notes/). `node tools/assets/verify-critical-attacks.mjs` checks the item document, critical spell and icon against the pinned source hashes.

Validation: 670 tests across 137 files pass. New coverage checks source consistency, unique item IDs, purchase/sale and restored chance, ordinary versus critical Lux damage, independent Illumination mitigation, windup PRNG continuation, projectile payload retention after sale, Ashe normal and Q damage/slow behavior, structure and zero-chance exclusions, and malformed checkpoint metadata. Five focused browser scenarios pass: both champion crit clips and checkpoint payloads, the actual shop purchase/icon/sale flow, Ashe Q reset, and ordinary Lux variant restoration. Nested-path production smoke passes. Native crit RNG, critical hit particles/audio, full critical-damage item interactions and frame-accurate native animation timing remain open.


## Infinity Edge and additive critical damage

The pinned 16.18 item catalogs agree on B. F. Sword (1,300 gold, 40 AD) and Infinity Edge (3,500 total gold, 75 AD, 25% crit chance). The client record supplies a flat critical-damage modifier of approximately 0.3, matching the displayed 30%. The modifier adds percentage points: Lux's critical attack multiplier becomes 2.3, rather than multiplying 2 by 1.3. Ashe's passive uses `1 + chance × (1 + bonus critical damage)`, producing 1.325 with Infinity Edge alone and 2.3 at 100% crit chance.

`basicAttackDamageMultiplier` centralizes these rules for release payloads and Ashe's guaranteed farming damage. Structure attacks continue to bypass the crit modifiers. Released projectiles retain their damage when Infinity Edge is sold. Q flurry splits the already modified attack damage through its existing five-shot calculation; the modifier does not multiply separate on-hit magic damage.

The recipe consumes B. F. Sword, Pickaxe and one Cloak of Agility, with a 725-gold combine cost when all three are owned. A full inventory can upgrade because consumed slots make room. Any additional Cloak is retained. Both referenced source ownership groups have a maximum of one; purchase and checkpoint validation reject duplicate Infinity Edges. The groups' hashed names are preserved without inventing labels or unsupported conflicts with unavailable items.

`reference/infinity-edge.json` preserves both source catalogs, their existing verified hashes, selected ownership groups, and icon hashes. `python3 tools/assets/export-critical-items.py --check` verifies generated catalog values, recipe agreement, tooltip agreement for crit damage and ownership groups. Item selling evidence includes both new source prices. No new checkpoint fields are needed; the existing simulation fingerprint changes with the rules.

Validation: 675 tests across 138 files pass. Five additional tests cover source/recipe consistency, a full-inventory upgrade with an extra Cloak, sale and restore, both champions' physical mitigation and launched damage persistence, the 100% chance boundary and duplicate-item import rejection. Four browser scenarios pass: existing Ashe/Lux critical attacks, Cloak purchase/sale, and Infinity Edge's actual full-inventory shop upgrade, source icon, browser reload and sale. The shop screenshot was visually reviewed. Nested-path production smoke passes. Native critical randomization, critical hit effects/audio, and the rest of the item catalog remain unfinished.

## Attack-stat inspection and interruption coverage

Hovering or focusing the HUD attack-damage value now exposes total critical strike chance and the relevant damage multiplier. Lux sees the ordinary critical multiplier; Ashe sees Frost Shot's guaranteed damage scaling and the explanation that critical rolls strengthen her slow. Values come from the same inventory-derived simulation methods used at release and update while the tooltip remains open. The tooltip states that values precede armor and exclude structures and separate on-hit effects. It uses the existing HUD tooltip styling; this is a usability addition, not a claim of native tooltip layout parity.

The owner supports focus, an `aria-describedby` relationship, a visible focus outline and Escape dismissal. Dismissing a focused tooltip does not open the pause menu. Opening a modal or losing window focus hides it, and placement is clamped to the viewport. Tests cover live Infinity Edge purchase/sale updates, hover/focus behavior, dismissal, modal hiding, normal Lux champion selection and 1120×720 bounds. The Ashe capture was visually reviewed.

Two additional simulation cases verify that a cancelled critical windup launches nothing, that the next attack receives a fresh roll from the saved PRNG state, and that a bot with shopping gold preserves its committed critical attack. All 681 tests across 139 files and both tooltip browser scenarios pass. Nested-path production smoke passes. Native critical randomization and hit effects remain unfinished.
