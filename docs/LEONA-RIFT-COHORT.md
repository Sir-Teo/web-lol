# Development Leona support match corpus

The immutable match runner accepts an explicit `--development-leona` option.
It creates the real Rift in the trusted development context and selects Leona
support through the existing lane factory. The normally human support is
controlled by the existing bot policy for QA. Both teams retain five roles:
six Ashe, three Lux and one Leona in total. Normal rules, source map, economy,
wave/camp calendars, visibility and victory dependencies remain unchanged.

The option propagates to each frozen child runner and its restored battlefields.
Plans and reports identify the development context and automated support role;
they do not label Leona as a carry. The ordinary default remains six Ashe/four
Lux with the human carry automated. A separate development hundred-seed gate
prevents this corpus from passing the ordinary roster gate. Both remain short
of the complete six-champion milestone.

Use a fresh output directory; existing evidence cannot be overwritten:

```sh
node tools/qa/match-cohort.mjs --development-leona --jobs 2 --minutes 10 --output .cache/leona-support-example 26018 719
node tools/qa/verify-match-cohort.mjs .cache/leona-support-example
```

The runner freezes its executable and map inputs, pins source digests, checks
resources and structure damage every tick, saves each minute and compares the
following 60 ticks against a restored world. It retains unfinished matches at
the requested horizon without changing clocks or forcing victory. Independent
verification checks the selected role and actual champion composition in saved
checkpoints, along with the existing invariant/future evidence. `--historical`
is required after relevant source changes.

## Initial scope and findings

Two ten-minute development runs, seeds 26018 and 719, pass independently verified
72,000 ticks, 20 strict round trips and 18 restored-future checks. Both remain
unfinished at the short horizon. A two-minute ordinary-default run passes 7,200
ticks, two round trips and one future comparison with its original carry role.
Neither smoke passes a hundred-seed or complete-match gate.

Leona has four and five deaths at ten minutes, respectively. These observations
prompt tactical investigation, not a balance conclusion. An observer replay of
seed 26018 matches an uninstrumented replay at each second and at the first
death, tick 6,944. It records 59 accepted damage calls from champions and caster
minions, with no turret damage. Before the first trade, ordinary melee pursuit
closes toward mobile ranged opponents while the allied carry remains outside
attack reach. The later retreat and death need to be evaluated with the full
trade, rather than inferred solely from minute samples. No combat policy was
changed for these baseline runs.

The longer two-seed baseline uses a 60-minute maximum. Both matches end in
structurally valid Red victories: seed 26018 at tick 115,729 (32:08.8) and seed
719 at tick 148,129 (41:08.8). Independent verification passes 263,858 checked
ticks, 75 strict round trips and 73 restored-future comparisons covering 4,380
ticks. Winning states remain frozen. Leona finishes with 12 and 16 deaths,
respectively, and no kills. This preserves a behavioral baseline for the next
local policy investigation. It does not establish balance, acceptable support
play or a hundred-seed gate.

Only QA tooling and documentation changed. The project TypeScript check passes;
all three corpora pass independent verification. Shipped ruleset
`fa137a2b473e83950a24b0825bb69083d2928fe9a687cb52c460b48a2e80e382` and
pack `e1cf99a2ff00880234df6ef96c6615284696cb675ae3c62f2a6edc83f6cd9d44`
remain unchanged from the accepted Rift trial. Exact sources, generated bytes,
corpora, executable/map pins and observer evidence are bound by the
[frozen ledger](reference/leona/rift-cohort-verification.json).
Native comparisons, three R families, full roster, support policy and the normal
match/performance matrix remain open.
