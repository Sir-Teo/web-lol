# Highlander takedown effects

The private Master Yi fixture renders the complete three-emitter
`MasterYi_R_OnBuffKill` system when a living caster actually receives an active
Highlander extension. Kills and assists use the existing award path. A passive
cooldown refund with no active R, or a posthumous refund, creates no effect.
Native script callback mapping remains unverified; this admission is the local
reconstruction's explicit choice.

The flash, mesh swirl and bubble retain their authored rates, finite lives,
rotation probabilities, spin, UV scroll, bind, render passes and blend controls.
Five packed textures carry all 42 authored mip levels. Two texture transports
and the 4,500-corner trail GLB are shared with Alpha Strike contacts. A page-owned
verified geometry cache shares the actual immutable geometry, rejects conflicting
asset declarations and evicts failed loads for explicit retry. Borrowing effect
instances do not dispose shared geometry.

The flash starts on the first local update. Continuous source emission gives
17 swirl births and bubble births at offsets 1 and 30 ticks. The second bubble
makes the finite saved bound 88 ticks. Source linger fields do not extend visible
particle lives. Neutral white for the omitted flash lookup, the first-update
emission grid, fixed cosmetic random draws, actor-root mapping and shader choices
are explicit local adapters, rather than native GPU certification. The source
system declares no on-create or persistent sound.

Each awarded extension records its R cast, rank, extension sequence, victim and
team, admission audience and lossless root pose/visibility history. Same-tick
kills remain separate. Q-hidden roots suppress births independently of admission;
a later bubble can appear after Q returns, without resurrecting hidden earlier
particles. Death freezes roots after Q cancellation; a known corpse root can therefore
show existing bound tails again. Terminal presentation drains finite tails without
advancing the match. Cosmetic sampling consumes no gameplay RNG or entity IDs.

Schema 157 validates the linked R admission and active/stopped extension count,
victim death clock/team, ordered unique identities, root coverage, endpoints and
death freeze. Saved histories preserve random seeks and future simulation.
Asset loading is lazy and retains verified successes across explicit retries.
The public production roster remains Ashe and Lux.
